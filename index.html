The Game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how players use the Game, and Firebase Crashlytics to find and fix crashes. These services may collect:

  • an app instance ID and a Firebase installation ID (random identifiers for this install of the Game);
  • usage events, such as first open, sessions, levels started, completed or failed, ads watched and purchases made, with timestamps;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, screen resolution, app version and country or region derived from your IP address;

We do not use Firebase to collect your name, email, contacts or precise location, and we do not combine Firebase data with advertising profiles. Google Analytics for Firebase may use your advertising ID only where you have allowed it and advertising features are permitted for your region.

2.3 Advertising (Yodo1 MAS and partners)

The Game uses the Yodo1 MAS (Managed Ad Services) SDK to show interstitial and rewarded ads. Yodo1 MAS mediates ads from networks including Google AdMob and AppLovin. When ads are requested, shown or tapped, these SDKs may collect:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID (AAID) and App Set ID;
  • IP address and approximate (city- or country-level) location derived from it;
  • device and network information, such as model, OS version, language, time zone, carrier and connection type;
  • app information, such as name, package name and version;
  • ad interaction data, such as ads requested, shown, clicked and whether a rewarded ad was completed;
  • diagnostic data related to ad delivery, such as SDK errors.

The Game does not request access to your precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

2.4 Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your card or bank details. We may receive limited transaction information, such as the product, an order ID and its status, so the Game can deliver your item.

2.6 This website

8 Deleting your data

The Game has no user accounts, so there is no account to delete. To remove your data:

  • Game data on your device: uninstall the Game, or go to Settings › Apps › Container Jam › Storage › Clear data.
  • Advertising data: delete or reset your advertising ID (Settings › Privacy › Ads). You can also ask each network to delete data it holds, using the links in section 5.
  • Firebase data and anything we hold: email alexandru.simion.1983@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data – Container Jam". Because Firebase identifiers are random and not linked to your name, it helps to tell us the date you installed the Game and your device model. We delete what we can identify within 30 days and confirm, and can forward your request to Yodo1 MAS. Uninstalling the Game also resets its Firebase identifiers, so no further data is linked to the old ones.
