Container Jam

Clear the harbour. Send every truck to the right ship before the berths fill up.

Get it on Google Play

Free to play on Android. Contains ads and optional in-app purchases.

Ships come in

Ships moor at the berths, each waiting for containers of one colour.

Trucks go out

Tap a coloured truck in the holding loop to send it to a matching ship. The crane does the loading.

Keep it moving

Plan your order so the berths never jam. Clear the yard to pass the level.

Story mode

100 hand-built levels, played in order. Your progress saves automatically on your device.

Random level

Jump into a random level, clear it and get a new one.

No account needed

No sign-up and no login. Just open the game and play.
